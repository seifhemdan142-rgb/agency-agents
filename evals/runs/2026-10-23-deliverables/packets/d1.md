# Export Compliance Procedure

**[Company Name] | Version 1.0 | Owner: Export Compliance Officer (ECO) | Effective: [date] | Reviewed annually**

## 1. Purpose and scope
As a US company, we are subject to US export controls (the EAR, administered by Commerce/BIS) and US sanctions (administered by Treasury/OFAC) wherever our customers are. This covers SaaS access, downloadable apps, SDKs/APIs, source code, and technical information shared with non-US persons. It applies to all employees, contractors, and resellers.

## 2. Roles
- **ECO** ([e.g., COO or GC]): owns this procedure, decides escalations, keeps records, engages outside counsel.
- **Sales/RevOps:** screens customers and partners before contract or activation.
- **Finance:** screens vendors and payees; flags payments tied to restricted locations.
- **Engineering:** maintains product classifications and geo-blocking.
- **People Ops:** flags non-US hires and contractors for access review.

## 3. Product classification
- Classify each product and major version. Because of encryption, most SaaS products are **5D992.c (mass market)** or **5D002 eligible for License Exception ENC**. Record the ECCN and the reasoning.
- Confirm whether any BIS encryption reporting (e.g., the annual self-classification report due Feb 1) applies to us.
- Re-classify when adding new cryptography, on-prem/downloadable versions, or specialized technical capabilities.

## 4. Restricted destinations (blocked by default)
- **Cuba, Iran, North Korea**, and the **Crimea, so-called DNR and LNR regions** of Ukraine.
- **Russia and Belarus**: extensive EAR restrictions, plus OFAC bans on certain IT and cloud-software services.
- **Heightened review:** Syria (rules changed substantially in 2025), Venezuela, and anything else the ECO adds. The ECO checks this list monthly.

**Controls:** IP geo-blocking at signup and login; block billing addresses, cards, and phone numbers from these locations; restrict app-store availability; monitor for VPN/proxy use from blocked regions.

## 5. Restricted-party screening
- Screen every customer (automated for self-serve; before signature for enterprise), reseller, vendor, and payee against the **Consolidated Screening List** (includes the OFAC SDN, BIS Entity, Denied Persons, Unverified, and Military End-User lists).
- Apply OFAC's **50% rule**: entities owned 50% or more, in aggregate, by blocked persons are blocked even if unlisted. Collect ownership information from enterprise customers.
- Re-screen the entire customer base automatically when lists update (at least weekly).
- **Potential match:** hold provisioning, escalate to the ECO within one business day, and don't tell the customer why until the ECO approves.

## 6. Red flags: stop and escalate
- Evasive answers about end user, end use, or ownership.
- Location, IP, and payment details that don't match.
- Uses connected to military, nuclear, missile, chemical/biological weapons, or surveillance activities.
- Military or government end users in China, Russia, or other countries of concern.
- Resellers with no identified end customer; unusual payment routing or third-party payers.

Never ignore red flags or avoid asking questions. "We didn't ask" is not a defense.

## 7. Internal access and deemed exports
Sharing controlled technology or source code with foreign nationals, even inside the US, can be an export. People Ops flags non-US hires and contractors, and the ECO confirms their access is permitted (it usually is for EAR99 or ENC-eligible technology). No employee or contractor may access our systems or code from a restricted destination. Check with the ECO before taking company laptops to a restricted destination.

## 8. Contracts
Terms of Service, order forms, and reseller agreements must include:
- an export/sanctions compliance clause;
- customer representations that it is not a restricted party, is not owned by one, and is not located in a restricted region;
- our right to suspend or terminate immediately.

Reseller agreements must also require resellers to screen their own customers.

## 9. Escalation and suspected violations
Report any suspected violation to the ECO immediately. The ECO brings in outside counsel. Preserve all records and do not delete accounts or data. The ECO and counsel decide on suspension, blocking or rejecting funds, required OFAC reports (blocked property must be reported within 10 business days), and voluntary self-disclosure to OFAC or BIS.
